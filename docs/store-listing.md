## Full description
```
Diganta (দিগন্ত) is a private, offline-first app for your money, time and goals — built for how
people actually live and spend in Bangladesh.

ONE APP FOR TAKA, TIME AND GOALS
• Track expenses and income in BDT (৳) with optional Bangla numerals
• Tag payments by Cash, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, Bank or Card
• Savings buckets: DPS, FDR, Bank, Committee/Somiti and cash at home
• Investments: stocks, mutual funds, gold, land, FDR and more, with gain/loss
• Budgets with alerts when you are close to or over a limit
• Bills and recurring payments with due reminders
• Debts and loans — see who owes you and what you owe
• Goals planner: set a target (e.g. buy a bike), and Diganta works out exactly how much to save
  each month and whether you are on track
• Tasks, habit streaks, health logs (weight, water, sleep, calories) and a daily routine
• Prayer times based on your location

AN AI COACH THAT LEARNS YOU
Diganta studies your own data — on your device — and gives personalised advice: your spending type,
a monthly savings target, overspend forecasts, category spikes, and simple ways to save more.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data is stored on your phone. Cloud backup is optional and protected by phone-number sign-in.
No ads, no selling your data.

BANGLA + ENGLISH
Full interface in Bangla and English, with BDT formatting and Bangla numerals.

Made for everyday life in Bangladesh.
```

## Data safety (Play Console) / App Privacy (App Store) answers
- **Collects data?** Only if the user enables optional cloud sync.
  - Data types: phone number (account), and app content (financial info) stored for the user.
  - Purpose: app functionality / account management.
  - Encrypted in transit: **Yes**.
  - User can request deletion: **Yes** (in-app erase + email).
- **Data shared with third parties?** No (except the infrastructure provider Supabase solely to
  store the user's own backup).
- **Data sold?** No.
- **Ads / tracking?** No.

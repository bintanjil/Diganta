# Diganta — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 10 September 2026

Diganta ("the app", "we") is a personal finance and lifestyle tracker. This policy explains what
data the app handles and how. We designed Diganta to be **private and offline-first**: your data
belongs to you and stays on your device by default.

## 1. Data you enter
Diganta stores the information you add — expenses, income, savings, investments, budgets, bills,
debts, goals, tasks, habits, health logs and routines — **locally on your device**. This data is not
sent to us and we cannot see it.

## 2. Cloud sync (optional, off by default)
If you choose to sign in to cloud sync, your data is backed up to our database provider
(**Supabase**) so you can restore it on another device. In that case:
- You sign in with your **phone number** and a one-time code (OTP).
- Your data is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection and stored under your account.
- Access is protected by row-level security so only your account can read or write your data.
- You can sign out at any time; you can also erase all data from **Coach → Data & backup**.

We do **not** sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising trackers.

## 3. Permissions the app may request
- **Camera / Photos** — only when you attach a receipt photo to an expense. The image is stored on
  your device.
- **Location** — only to calculate accurate prayer times. Location is used on your device and is
  not transmitted to us.
- **Notifications** — to send bill-due and daily check-in reminders. You can turn these off anytime.

## 4. Diagnostics
The app does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs by default.

## 5. Data retention and deletion
Local data remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Cloud data (if you enabled sync)
remains until you erase it in the app or request deletion. To request deletion, contact us at the
email below.

## 6. Children
Diganta is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## 7. Security
We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, access controls). No method of transmission
or storage is 100% secure, so please keep your device and backups safe.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date.

## 9. Contact
Questions or deletion requests: **support@diganta.app**
